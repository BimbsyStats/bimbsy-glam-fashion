Our story

Creative fashion, made with care.

Bimbsy Glam Fashion is a Lagos-based fashion brand built around creativity, self-expression and the little details that make an outfit feel special.

We create ready-to-wear looks and accessories, sew by commission, and offer fashion sketching and illustration for customers who need help developing an outfit idea. From sketch to pattern and fabric to finished garment, we bring creativity and care to each agreed project.
